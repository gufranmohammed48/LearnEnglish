Angrezi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Angrezi is a free app that teaches English to Urdu speakers. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, lessons completed, points, streak and the words you have learned are saved only on your device. We have no account system and no server that stores your progress. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) deletes this information.

Microphone and speech

Speaking exercises use your phone's or browser's built-in speech recognition. Audio is processed by that service (for example Google on Android, Apple on iPhone, or your browser's provider) only while you hold the microphone button, so the app can compare what you said with the sentence. Angrezi does not record, save or send your voice anywhere itself. You can turn speaking exercises off in Settings.

Notifications

If you choose a daily reminder time, the reminder is scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to us.

Fonts

The website loads the Noto Nastaliq Urdu and Nunito fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of any normal web request.

Advertising

Angrezi does not show ads yet. If ads are added later, they will be provided by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. You will be asked for consent where the law requires it, and this policy will be updated before ads are turned on.

Children

Angrezi is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/gufranmohammed48/LearnEnglish/issues.
